t2m: Japan box-mean t-test (σ = 1.4°C, n_eff = n/5)  
tp: Best same-sign sub-region t-test (n_eff = n/5); region noted in parentheses

## Significance methodology note

| Variable | Method | σ_box | DOF correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| t2m | Japan box-mean two-sided t-test | 1.4°C (back-calculated from documented SE) | n_eff = n/5 (MJO 5-day autocorrelation) |
| tp | Best same-sign sub-region two-sided t-test | ~4.4 mm/day (gridpoint σ / √6) | n_eff = n/5 |

- tp sub-region test is unadjusted for multiple comparisons (best of 5 regions). Reflects local signal in W./E. Japan.
- No formally significant cells in El Niño or La Niña layers (n = 28–89 too small).
- Map stippling (script 07) uses gridpoint-level tests, which cover more area than the box-average test.
